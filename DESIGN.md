# Skul Design

## Direction

The landing page helps developers understand how one AI configuration bundle travels across projects and tools, then reach a first successful install. The selected direction uses a white canvas, editorial serif headings, restrained blue accents, and dark terminal surfaces. Preserve the existing ghost logo and use real commands and supported capabilities.

[Selected visual direction](assets/design/landing-direction.png) (concept only; generated commands and claims are not authoritative).

## Visual rules

- White background, near-black ink `#151b24`, muted text `#526071`, and blue `#0868e8`; dark terminal surfaces ground product examples.
- Serif display headings contrast with sans-serif body text and monospace commands. Use generous spacing and light borders to separate the story.
- Use the existing logo as the ghost identity; avoid introducing a competing mascot.

## Flow and components

The landing tells one story in order: promise and example, bundle reuse, team and personal instructions, clean repository, and first steps. Links lead to installation, quick start, supported tools, and technical details. Diagrams support the text and do not require interaction.

## Responsive behavior and accessibility

At 996px and below, stack two-column sections; at 750px and below, stack the bundle diagram; at 600px and below, arrange tool cards in a single column and reduce display sizes. Preserve visible link focus, meaningful link labels, readable contrast, and a usable layout without animation. Terminal examples can scroll horizontally.
